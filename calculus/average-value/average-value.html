average value/height/elevation of a fxn

What do you think the average value/height/elevation of a lobe of a sine function is (e.g., the chunk of sine between zero and pi, or of cosine between \(-\pi/2\) and \(+\pi/2\)). Make a guess! And then, figure it out, precisely/algebraically/analytically!!!

other cool followup: we figured out the average value of \(-x^2 + 9\) between its roots. it’s \(6\)—a little more than halfway up! what about, similarly, the average value of \(y = - x^4 + 16\) between ITS roots? is it halfway between the top and the bottom? more? less? more more than \(-x^3 + 9\) ???

likewise: consider the shape made by \(y=\sqrt(x)\) on the top, the horizontal/\(y\)-axis on the bottom, and the line \(x=16\) on the right. draw it!!! what do you think its average elevation is? then calculate it!
